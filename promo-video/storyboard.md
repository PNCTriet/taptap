## Khung 1 – Chạm NFC (2.5–4.5s)
Clip đã duyệt: clips/f1_nfc_tap_v1.mp4 (hoặc take sạch hơn), cắt ~2s, flash trắng chuyển sang Khung 2.
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 storyboard frame, [STYLE]. Close-up of a young Gen Z hand (stylized, simple flat shapes, short nails, a pastel scrunchie on the wrist) holding a smartphone and tapping it against a small round wooden NFC post sign at a zoo, the post decorated with a simple leaf-shaped animal paw icon (no text). Concentric tap ripples radiate from the contact point, a bright white flash beginning to bloom from the phone screen. Background: blurred-flat zoo path with tropical leaves and a soft sunny sky. Composition: phone and post meet at the vertical center, upper third clean for later overlay. No text, no logos.
Chuyển động: tay đưa vào từ dưới phải (ease-out 0.4s) → chạm, phone squash nhẹ 5% → 3 vòng ripple scale 0→1.5 opacity 1→0 → flash trắng full màn 2 frame ở ~4.4s.
SFX: whoosh ngắn, "tik" NFC chirp, sparkle riser, flash "pshh".
16:9: đặt tay + trụ lệch trái 1/3, bên phải để trống cho text/hiệu ứng.

## Khung 2 – Mở thẻ (4.5–12s)
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 storyboard frame, [STYLE]. A single portrait collectible animal card in the exact center mid-flip, shown at a 3D perspective angle (about 35° rotation on the Y axis), its back side (simple paw-and-leaf pattern) turning to reveal the front: a cute capybara illustration with a holographic foil sheen streak sweeping diagonally across the card. Behind it, a powerful radial burst of light rays, glowing sparkles and small star particles exploding outward. A faint ring of light on the ground below like a summoning circle. No text, no logos.
Chuyển động: thẻ úp bay lên từ đáy, rung nhẹ 3 nhịp (build-up ~1s) → lật Y 180° (0.5s, ease back) → burst tia scale + rotate chậm, foil shine quét chéo 2 lần, con vật trên thẻ nảy squash-stretch → giữ hero 2s, camera zoom chậm 105%.
SFX: rung "brrr", drum roll ngắn, card flip "fwip", impact boom + chime sáng, sparkle twinkle loop.
16:9: thẻ ở giữa, burst trải ngang rộng hơn, thêm 2 thẻ mờ hai bên làm depth.

## Khung 3 – Chuyển cảnh sang Collection (12–16s)
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 storyboard frame, [STYLE]. A card flying horizontally across the screen from left to right with motion-blur speed lines, leaving a trail of sparkles; behind it, a collection screen being revealed: a neat 3x5 grid of 15 rounded card slots on a clean phone-UI-like panel, each showing a cute simple animal portrait (elephant, giraffe, capybara, meerkat, sun bear, red panda, white rhino, tiger, yellow-cheeked gibbon, flamingo, peacock, crocodile, sulcata tortoise, pony, pygmy hippo), some slots filled in color and a few still as grey silhouettes. No text, no logos.
Chuyển động: thẻ wipe ngang (0.6s) làm mask reveal màn collection → 15 ô pop-in stagger 40ms/ô (scale 0→1.1→1) → ô capybara vừa mở sáng lên + check bounce.
SFX: swoosh lớn, chuỗi "pop pop pop" tăng cao độ, "ding" khi ô sáng.
16:9: lưới đổi thành 5x3, thẻ bay chéo từ trái dưới lên phải trên.

## Khung 4 – Quiz + kinetic text (16–22s)
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 storyboard frame, [STYLE]. A playful quiz moment: a rounded speech-bubble question card at the top with a cute meerkat peeking from its edge, three chunky rounded answer buttons below (blank, no text), one button glowing green with a check burst. In the middle, big bold bouncy display letters reading "TAP! COLLECT! WIN!" stacked in three lines, each letter slightly rotated and at a different height as if bouncing, with thick outlines and a drop shadow. Confetti dots around. No other text, no logos.
Chuyển động: câu hỏi slide-down + meerkat ló ra nháy mắt → nút đúng sáng, burst check → chữ TAP! / COLLECT! / WIN! xuất hiện từng ký tự, mỗi ký tự spring (y offset -40→0, overshoot 1.2, stagger 35ms), mỗi từ đổi màu theo bảng màu, cả cụm giật nhẹ theo nhịp nhạc.
SFX: "boing" cho từng từ, đúng đáp án "ding-ding", nhạc beat drop nhẹ.
16:9: quiz bên trái, khối chữ bên phải xếp 1 hàng ngang.
Lưu ý: chữ dựng trong Remotion (font thật), ảnh Gemini chỉ để tham khảo layout.

## Khung 5 – Huy hiệu lên hạng (22–27s)
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 storyboard frame, [STYLE]. Three newly designed achievement badges in a vertical progression with glowing arrows between them: top a small round bronze badge with an egg resting on a nest of leaves, middle a larger silver badge with a cracked egg sprouting a green seedling, bottom the largest gold holographic badge with a blooming tropical tree, laurel wings and a radiant glow. Original badge illustrations only (not animal card art). Confetti and ribbon streamers bursting around the gold badge. No text, no numbers, no logos.
Chuyển động: badge đồng pop-in → morph/scale-up thành bạc (trứng nứt, mầm mọc bằng path animation) → thành vàng holo (cây mọc + hoa nở stagger), shine quét, confetti 2 đợt.
SFX: "clink" đồng, "shing" bạc, fanfare ngắn + confetti pop khi vàng.
16:9: 3 huy hiệu xếp ngang trái → phải, mũi tên ngang.
